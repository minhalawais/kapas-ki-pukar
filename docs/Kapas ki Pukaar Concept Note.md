


## Concept Note
Kapas ki Pukaar (کپاس کی پکار )
A Voice-Enabled Smart Grievance Reporting Application for Cotton Farm Workers
## BACKGROUND  AND RATIONALE
Pakistan's cotton sector supports millions of rural households and remains one of the country's most
important agricultural value chains. Cotton production relies heavily on seasonal workers, women
cotton pickers,  tenant farmers and migrant labourers who often work under informal arrangements
with limited access to grievance mechanisms and labour protection.
Many  workers  experience  delayed  wages,  unsafe  pesticide  exposure,  excessive  working  hours,
discrimination,  harassment, child labour risks, and inadequate occupational safety. Despite these
challenges, workers rarely report violations due to fear of retaliation, low literacy, limited awareness
of their rights, and lack of accessible reporting channels.
Digital  technology  offers  an  opportunity  to  bridge  this  gap.  However,  conventional  mobile
applications depend on reading and typing, making them difficult for many agricultural workers to
use.  A worker-centred  solution must  therefore  be  simple,  conversational  and available  in  local
languages.
## PROPOSED CONCEPT
Kapas ki Pukaar (کپاس کی پکار) is an innovative, voice-enabled mobile
application that transforms grievance reporting into a natural
conversation.

Unlike traditional complaint applications where users fill lengthy forms, Kapas ki Pukaar
talks directly to workers.
The application greets the worker in Urdu and asks simple questions one by one using
voice prompts. Instead of typing, the worker only listens and selects the most suitable
answer from easy-to-understand options displayed with icons and illustrations.
For example, the application asks:
"السلام علیکم، میں کپاس کی پکار ہوں۔ کیا آپ کو کام سے متعلق کوئی مسئلہ پیش آیا ہے؟"
The worker simply taps "ہاں" or "نہیں".


The application then continues asking relevant questions such as:
- What happened?
- Where did it happen?
- When did it happen?
- Is anyone currently in danger?
- Would you like to remain anonymous?
Based on the worker's selected responses, the application automatically prepares a
structured grievance report for review by competent authorities.
This conversational approach removes the need for reading lengthy forms or writing
detailed complaints, making the system accessible even for workers with limited literacy.




## OBJECTIVES
The objective of this initiative is to:
- Provide a safe, confidential and worker-friendly  grievance reporting platform for cotton farm
workers.
- Enable low-literacy workers to report workplace issues through guided voice conversations.
- Increase  reporting  of  labour  rights  violations  through  an  intuitive  question-and-answer
process.
- Strengthen  access  to  remedy  by  connecting  workers  with  appropriate  grievance
management systems.

## CONCLUSION
Kapas  ki  Pukaar represents  a  new  generation  of  worker-centred  digital  grievance
mechanisms. Rather than expecting workers to navigate complex forms, the application
communicates  with them in simple language, asks relevant questions, and guides them
through the entire reporting process.
By combining conversational technology, voice assistance, suggested responses and
secure case management, Kapas ki Pukaar ensures that every cotton worker—regardless
of literacy level—has a safe opportunity to raise concerns and seek remedy.
More than a complaint application, Kapas ki Pukaar is a digital companion that listens first,
asks the right questions, and helps workers find their voice. Its innovative design has the
potential to transform labour grievance reporting across Pakistan's cotton sector and can
later be adapted for other agricultural value chains.ss
